# Exercise substitutions, cues and prehab

The two constraints that shape every block: a grumpy knee and a shoulder that objects to
pressing followed by pulling. Substitutions below are ordered from most to least preferred.

## Knee

| Removed / risky | Replacement | Cue that keeps it safe |
|---|---|---|
| Болгарский сплит | Жим ногами одной ногой | Weight per leg, short ROM, foot flat and comfortable, no chase for depth |
| Выпады с длинным шагом | Hip thrust / ягодичный мост | 2 s pause at top, ribs down, lower back not overextended |
| Глубокий присед со штангой | Жим ногами, стопы на ширине таза | 3-1-1 tempo, knees track over feet, no bounce at the bottom, no lockout under load |
| Разгибание ног с большим весом | Сгибание ног лёжа | 3 s eccentric — hamstrings load without patellar compression |
| — | Румынская тяга, становая с трап-грифом | Hips back, knees fixed, neutral spine |
| — | Изометрия у стены, если колено разболелось | 45–60°, 3×30 s — replaces leg press for 1–2 weeks |

Progression on lower body goes through volume and tempo first, +5–10 kg second; leg press
climbing 10 kg a week is too fast for a knee that talks back.

## Shoulder

| Removed / risky | Replacement | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Тяжёлый штанговый жим 2×/нед | Один жим штанги + один жим гантелей нейтральным хватом | Elbows 45–60°, shoulder blades down and back, bar to lower chest |
| Подтягивания / верхняя тяга широким хватом | Тяга верхнего блока параллельным хватом | Pull to upper chest, no torso swing, blades down |
| Тяга штанги в наклоне при боли | Тяга гантели с опорой на скамью | One arm, neutral grip, free hand braces the bench |
| Жим в отказ | Жим с паузой 2 s, 70–80% | Pause kills the bounce; technique sets, not grinders |
| — | Face pull | To the face, elbows above wrists, 3×15 every week |

## Daily prehab (5 min, non-negotiable)

Band external rotation 2×15 · face pull 2×15 · scapular push-ups 2×10 · doorway chest stretch 2×30 s.
Scheduled in the plan as warm-up content so it is not optional.

## Test protocol

Bench 1RM: 40×5, 60×3, 75×1, 87,5×1, 92,5–95×1 — spotter or rack pins mandatory; if 95 moved fast
and pain-free, 97,5 with safety only. Row 5RM for load calibration; skip it entirely if the shoulder
is symptomatic and keep the last working weight.

## Red flags — refer out, don't program around

Pain >3/10 during or after, night pain, swelling, morning stiffness >30 min, clicking accompanied by
pain, weakness or the joint giving way. Say it in the plan and in chat; suggest an orthopedist or
physiotherapist rather than another substitution.
